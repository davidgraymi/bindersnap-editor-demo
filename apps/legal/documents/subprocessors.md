# Subprocessors

Last updated: October 7, 2026
Version: 2026-10-07

A subprocessor is a company we use to run Bindersnap that may handle personal information on our behalf. We keep the list short and use each company only for what's listed here.

All Customer Content (your organization's documents, versions, approvals, reviews, comments, reactions and tags) is stored only on our servers at Amazon Web Services in the United States. The other companies below never receive Customer Content.

## Current subprocessors

| Name                       | What they do for us                                                                                             | Data they process                                                                                                                                                            | Location                           |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Amazon Web Services, Inc.  | Hosts our servers, storage and backups (EC2, EBS, S3); keeps server logs (CloudWatch); delivers our email (SES) | All Customer Content; account, billing and configuration data; server logs including usernames and IP addresses; email addresses and message contents for the emails we send | USA (us-east-1, Northern Virginia) |
| Stripe, Inc.               | Subscription billing and payments                                                                               | Your organization's ID; the username of the person who subscribed; billing details and payment cards entered directly on Stripe's pages (Bindersnap never sees card numbers) | USA                                |
| Automattic Inc. (Gravatar) | Profile pictures                                                                                                | A SHA-256 hash of the account's email address, and the IP address of the person viewing the picture                                                                          | USA                                |
| GitHub, Inc.               | Hosts our public website and the app's files                                                                    | IP addresses and browser details of visitors. No Customer Content.                                                                                                           | USA                                |
| Google LLC (Google Fonts)  | Supplies web fonts for our public website and help pages                                                        | IP addresses and browser details of visitors. No Customer Content.                                                                                                           | USA                                |

Gravatar, GitHub and Google Fonts receive information straight from the visitor's browser, not from our servers. Our Privacy Policy at [/legal/privacy](/legal/privacy) explains this in more detail.

## When this list changes

Before we add or replace a subprocessor, we will:

1. update this page and its "Last updated" date, and
2. email the owners of every organization at least 30 days before the new subprocessor starts handling personal information.

If your organization objects to a new subprocessor, follow the objection process in Section 6 of our Data Processing Addendum at [/legal/dpa](/legal/dpa).
