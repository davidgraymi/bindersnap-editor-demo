# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026
Version: 2026-10-09

This policy explains what personal information Bindersnap collects, why we collect it, who else receives it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

The short version: we collect what we need to run Bindersnap and nothing more. In the app we use one cookie, only to keep you signed in, and we run no analytics and no ads. On the public pages of our website written for people choosing software, such as pricing and our policy templates, we use Google Analytics to learn which pages help; it never runs in the app. The app's fonts and profile pictures come from us, not from outside services. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. Your organization's documents belong to your organization, and we handle them only to provide the service.

## 2. Our two roles

Organizations use Bindersnap to keep their documents in binders. Every version, approval, review comment and published version is kept as a permanent audit record. That gives us two different roles.

**For your organization's content, we work for your organization.** Under the GDPR, your organization is the "controller" and we are its "processor." Under US state privacy laws, we are its "service provider" or "processor." This covers:

- documents, whether uploaded (Word, PDF and similar files) or written in the Bindersnap editor
- versions, approvals, reviews, review comments, reactions and tags
- the information your organization adds about its people: invitations (by email address), team membership and roles in each binder

We call all of this "Customer Content." Your organization decides what goes into its binders, who can see them, and when they are deleted. Our agreement with your organization, including our Data Processing Addendum at [/legal/dpa](/legal/dpa), controls how we handle Customer Content. If you have a question about Customer Content, start with your organization. If you ask us, we will pass the request to them (see section 12).

**For everything else, we are responsible.** We are the controller (or "business," under California law) for:

- your Bindersnap account
- billing and subscriptions
- our website
- the emails we send you
- security and server logs

Most of this policy covers that second group.

**One thing to know before you start.** Once you review, comment on, approve or publish a document, that action is part of your organization's permanent record. It stays there, with your name, even if you later delete your account. A tamper-evident record only works if past entries can't be removed. Your organization needs that record as evidence.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

**One cookie.** When you sign in, we set a single cookie called `bindersnap_session`. It keeps you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly, so scripts on the page cannot read it. It is strictly necessary: Bindersnap cannot work without it. That's why we don't show a cookie banner. Signing out ends your session on our server.

In the app we set no other cookies. We don't use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or social-media trackers there.

**Analytics cookies on our marketing pages.** On the public pages of our website written for people choosing software (such as /pricing, /templates and /for), Google Analytics sets its own cookies, named `_ga` and `_ga_` followed by an ID, to tell one visit from another. We have turned off Google signals and ad personalization, so these visits aren't used for advertising. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, those pages don't load Google Analytics at all. You can also block or delete these cookies in your browser settings. They are never set in the app, on our home page, or on our help and legal pages.

**Browser storage on your device.** The app also saves two things in your browser's local storage:

- your theme choice (for example, light or dark)
- AutoRecover text: a copy of what you've typed in the editor but haven't saved yet, so it isn't lost if your browser closes

Both stay on your device. We don't collect them from there. You can clear them in your browser settings. If you use a shared computer, keep in mind that unsaved editor text stays on that computer until it is saved or cleared.

## 5. Who receives your information

We use a small number of companies to run Bindersnap. The full list, with what each one does and where, is at [/legal/subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors), along with our website analytics provider, which isn't a subprocessor. In short:

- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** hosts our servers, storage, backups and logs, and sends our emails. Customer Content is stored with AWS in the United States.
- **Stripe** handles subscription billing and payments.
- **Cloudflare** runs the network in front of our servers and hosts our public website and the app's files. Every request passes through it, so it sees your IP address and the content of your requests in transit. It also stores an encrypted copy of our backups, which it cannot read, and forwards email sent to our @bindersnap.com addresses.
- **Google** provides Google Analytics on our public marketing pages. It receives the details in the website analytics row of section 3 for people who read those pages. It never receives Customer Content or account data, and it isn't loaded in the app.

**Fonts and profile pictures come from us.** Our fonts are served from our own website, so loading a page doesn't contact Google or any other font service. Profile pictures are patterns our own server draws from each username. We don't use Gravatar or any other outside picture service, and no hash of your email address leaves Bindersnap.

**Inside your organization.** People in your organization can see your name, username and profile picture, and what you've done in the binders they can access.

**When the law requires it.** We may disclose information when a valid legal process requires it. We may also disclose it when we need to protect the rights, property or safety of Bindersnap, our customers or others. Where the law allows, we will tell your organization first.

**If the business changes hands.** If Solid Gray LLC is merged, acquired or sells Bindersnap, your information may pass to the new owner. This policy will continue to apply to it, and we will tell you before anything changes.

## 6. We don't sell or share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as California law uses that word. We do not use it for targeted advertising. We have not done any of these in the past 12 months.

There is nothing to opt out of. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out anyway, and our marketing pages don't load Google Analytics.

## 7. Email

We send only emails that are part of using Bindersnap:

- confirming your email address
- invitations to join an organization
- password resets
- notifications about changes in your binders: for example, when someone requests your review, asks for changes, or when a document is ready or published

You can turn notification types on or off in your settings. Every notification email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can't turn off email confirmation, invitations or password resets, because they are needed to use and protect your account.

We don't send marketing email from the app. If we ever want to send product news, we will ask for your permission first.

Amazon SES (part of AWS) delivers our email. Email you send to an @bindersnap.com address passes through Cloudflare, which forwards it to us.

## 8. How long we keep information

**Your account.** We keep your account until you delete it. You can delete it yourself in your settings. When you do:

- your account is deleted
- your sessions end
- your email-verification record is removed
- drafts you never proposed are retired so no one can pick them up. They stay in your organization's binder but are no longer working drafts.
- your reviews, comments and published versions stay in your organization's record, as explained in section 2

You can't delete your account while you are the only owner of an organization. Add another owner, or delete the organization, first.

**Your agreement to our Terms.** We keep the record of which version you agreed to, and when, for six years after your account is deleted, so we can show what was agreed if a question comes up within the time the law allows for one. Then we delete it. It holds your username, your account's ID, the organization you created at signup (if any), the version and the date, nothing else.

**Your organization's binders.** Your organization decides how long its binders exist. Only organization owners can delete a binder, and they must type its name to confirm. Deleting a binder permanently removes every version, approval and discussion in it. Before that, Bindersnap offers to download each document's audit packet. A settings-history entry stays to show that the binder was deleted.

**Your organization.** Owners can delete an organization once it has no binders and no subscription still charging. This removes its members, groups and name. We keep one minimal record, renamed "[name] (deleted)", so that a free trial can't be restarted by deleting and recreating the organization.

**Server logs.** 30 days.

**Website analytics.** Google Analytics keeps the data from our marketing pages for 14 months, then deletes it.

**Billing records.** As long as tax and accounting law requires. Stripe keeps its own records under its own policy.

**Messages you send us.** As long as we need to answer you, and to show we handled any privacy request.

**Backups.** Deleted information stays in our backups until they age out: up to 2 days in hourly disk snapshots, up to 35 days in daily disk snapshots, up to 30 days in our database backups, and up to 45 days in our encrypted off-site backups. We don't restore deleted information from backups, except to recover from a failure.

**Exports.** Your organization can download document audit packets and PDF or Word copies, even if its subscription has lapsed. Our Terms of Service explain how long that lasts.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of these rights. We offer the core rights to everyone, wherever you live.

- **Access.** Ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
- **Portability.** Get your information in a common, machine-readable format.
- **Correction.** Ask us to fix information that's wrong. You can change your name yourself in settings.
- **Deletion.** Ask us to delete your information. You can also delete your account yourself in settings.
- **Restriction and objection (GDPR).** Ask us to limit how we use your information, or object to uses based on our legitimate interests.
- **Withdraw consent.** Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.
- **Opt out of selling, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling (US states).** We don't do any of these, so there's nothing to turn off.
- **Limit use of sensitive personal information (California).** The only sensitive information we hold is your sign-in details (username and password), and we use them only to sign you in. So there's nothing to limit.
- **Equal treatment.** We won't treat you differently or charge you more for using your rights.
- **Appeal (US states).** If we turn down your request, you can ask us to reconsider (see section 12).
- **Complain.** You can complain to a data protection authority where you live or work. In the US, you can contact your state Attorney General. We'd appreciate the chance to fix the problem first.

**Limits.** Some rights have limits under the law. For example, we can't remove your name from your organization's audit record, because that record is your organization's evidence and is controlled by them. We will always tell you why if we can't do something.

## 12. How to make a request

Email [privacy@bindersnap.com](mailto:privacy@bindersnap.com). Tell us what you'd like, and send it from the email address on your account if you can.

- **Checking it's you.** We will confirm your identity before we act, usually by email to your account address. We may ask for more if we need it. We use that information only to verify you.
- **Someone acting for you.** An authorized agent can make a request for you. We may ask for proof that you gave them permission, and ask you to confirm your identity directly.
- **Timing.** We reply within one month under the GDPR, and within 45 days under US state laws. If we need more time, as the law allows, we will tell you why.
- **Appeals.** If we turn down your request, reply with "appeal" in the subject line. We will review it and answer within the time your state's law allows. If you still disagree, you can contact your state Attorney General.

**Requests about Customer Content.** If your request is about documents, reviews, comments or other content in an organization's binders, that organization controls it. We will pass your request to the organization and help them answer it. We won't act on it ourselves without their instruction.
