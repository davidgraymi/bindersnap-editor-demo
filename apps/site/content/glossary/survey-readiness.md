**Survey readiness** is being able to pass a survey on any day, not just in the weeks before one is expected. Medicare and accreditation surveys of facilities are typically unannounced, so the policy manual, the staff and the records have to be right all the time.

## What surveyors check in the policy manual

1. **The policy exists** where a rule requires one in writing.
2. **It's current**, and reviewed on the cycle your rules and your own policy on policies set. See [how often policies must be reviewed](/requirements/how-often-to-review-policies).
3. **It was approved** by the right people, and you can show who and when. See [who has to approve healthcare policies](/requirements/who-approves-policies).
4. **Staff know it** and can find it.
5. **Staff follow it.** Surveyors watch practice and compare it with the written policy.
6. **You can show history**: what the policy said on a past date, when following up on a complaint or an incident.

## A short readiness checklist

- Every policy shows its effective date, last review date and approvers.
- No policy is past its review date.
- Exactly one current version of each policy exists, and staff know where to find it.
- Superseded versions are kept, with the dates they were in force.
- Required plans, such as emergency preparedness and the exposure control plan, are within their review cycles.
