**Conditions for Coverage (CfCs)** are the federal requirements that a Medicare _supplier_ must meet to participate in the Medicare program. CMS defines them as "the requirements suppliers must meet to participate in the Medicare program" ([42 CFR 488.1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-488.1)).

## CfCs vs. CoPs

The difference is in the Medicare category, not in how demanding the rules are. Providers, such as home health agencies and hospices, meet [Conditions of Participation](/glossary/conditions-of-participation). Suppliers, such as ambulatory surgery centers, meet Conditions for Coverage. Both are surveyed the same way, and both lead to a [statement of deficiencies](/glossary/statement-of-deficiencies) when a surveyor finds a problem.

## The ASC Conditions for Coverage

For ambulatory surgery centers, the CfCs are in [42 CFR Part 416, Subpart C](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-416/subpart-C). They start with the governing body, which "assumes full legal responsibility for determining, implementing, and monitoring policies governing the ASC's total operation" (§416.41). They go on to cover surgical services, quality assessment and performance improvement, the environment, medical staff, nursing, medical records, pharmaceutical services, laboratory and radiology, patient rights, infection control, patient admission and discharge, and emergency preparedness.

## What they mean for your policy manual

Surveyors use the CfCs as the checklist. For each condition, they read your policy, watch your staff, and ask who approved the policy and when. Our [page for ambulatory surgery centers](/for/ambulatory-surgery-centers) lists what each condition usually turns into on paper.
