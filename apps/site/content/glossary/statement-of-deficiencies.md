A **statement of deficiencies** is the written list of the requirements a surveyor found a facility did not meet. For Medicare surveys it is recorded on **Form CMS-2567**, "Statement of Deficiencies and Plan of Correction". The form's instructions say the facility's signed form, with its proposed corrective action, must be returned to the surveying agency **within 10 days of receipt**.

## What's on it

Each deficiency names the requirement, usually by its tag and regulation, followed by the surveyor's findings: what they reviewed, observed or were told that shows the requirement wasn't met. Many findings cite a policy, either because the facility had no written policy where one is required, or because staff did not follow the policy it had.

## What to do when you receive one

1. Read each finding against the regulation it cites.
2. Find the policy involved, and the version that was in force on the day of the survey.
3. Write a [plan of correction](/glossary/plan-of-correction) for each deficiency, on the same form, and return it signed within the deadline in your cover letter.

Your state agency's letter sets your actual deadline, and it may differ from the federal default.

## Why version history matters here

A finding often turns on what the policy said at the time. If the policy has since been saved over, you can't show it. Bindersnap keeps every version with the date it took effect and who approved it, so you can show the version that applied on the survey date and the revision that corrects the finding.
