Rural health clinics and federally qualified health centers run lean. The person who keeps the policy manual is often also the office manager, the compliance officer and the one who answers the phone. When the state surveyor comes for the recertification visit, that person needs to show that the patient care policies were reviewed by the right group, on time, and that staff follow the current version.

Bindersnap keeps every policy with its current approved version, every earlier version, and the names and dates of each review and approval.

## What 42 CFR Part 491 says

RHCs and FQHCs meet the requirements in [42 CFR Part 491, Subpart A](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-491/subpart-A). Section 491.9(b) is the one about policies, and it's specific:

- Health care services are furnished "in accordance with appropriate written policies which are consistent with applicable State law."
- The policies are developed with the advice of a **group of professional personnel** that includes one or more physicians and one or more physician assistants or nurse practitioners, and **at least one member who is not on the clinic's staff**.
- The policies include a description of the services the clinic furnishes directly and through agreements, guidelines for the medical management of health problems, and rules for storing, handling and administering drugs and biologicals.
- The policies "are reviewed at least biennially by the group of professional personnel" and reviewed as necessary by the clinic.

Two more sections reach the policy manual. Section 491.11 requires a **biennial evaluation of the total program**, and that evaluation includes a review of "the clinic's or center's health care policies" to determine whether "the established policies were followed." Section 491.12 requires an emergency preparedness plan that is "reviewed and updated at least every 2 years."

## What a surveyor will ask for

| Requirement                                          | Evidence that answers it                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Written patient care policies (§491.9(b)(1))         | The current policy manual                          |
| Developed with the professional group (§491.9(b)(2)) | Who was on the group, including the outside member |
| Reviewed at least biennially (§491.9(b)(4))          | The date of the last review and who took part      |
| Biennial program evaluation (§491.11)                | The evaluation, including the policies reviewed    |
| Emergency preparedness (§491.12)                     | The plan and its policies, updated within 2 years  |

## Frequently asked questions

### How often must rural health clinic policies be reviewed?

At least every two years. Under 42 CFR §491.9(b)(4), the patient care policies are reviewed at least biennially by the group of professional personnel, and as necessary by the clinic. The biennial program evaluation under §491.11 also reviews the policies.

### Who has to be in the RHC professional group?

At least one physician, at least one physician assistant or nurse practitioner, and at least one person who is not a member of the clinic's staff (§491.9(b)(2)).

### Do the same rules apply to FQHCs?

Section 491.9(b) applies to both RHCs and FQHCs. FQHCs funded under Section 330 also answer to HRSA's Health Center Program requirements, which have their own expectations for board-approved policies.
