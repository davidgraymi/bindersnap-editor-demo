PolicyTech is one of the best-known names in policy management. If you're a small healthcare provider looking at it, you're weighing a mature compliance platform built for organizations of every size against what you need: one place for your policies, a record of who approved each version, and an answer ready for the surveyor. Here is an honest comparison, including where PolicyTech does more.

_Based on NAVEX's public website as of October 8, 2026. Products change, so check their site for current details._

## First, the name

NAVEX has renamed PolicyTech **NAVEX One Policy & Procedure Management**. Its site still calls it "formerly NAVEX PolicyTech" and lists it in the menu as "Policy & Procedure Management (PolicyTech)". It is one product in the NAVEX One compliance platform, alongside NAVEX's training, risk and incident management products.

## The short version

- **Choose PolicyTech** if you need policy management **together with** staff attestations, linked training, an AI assistant that answers employees' policy questions, and the rest of a compliance platform, and you are ready for a sales-led purchase.
- **Choose Bindersnap** if you want a policy manual where every policy has its approved version, its full history and its approvals on record, set up yourself without a sales call, at $39 a month for each person who writes policies.

## Where PolicyTech does more

PolicyTech is a broader product, and NAVEX has been building it for a long time. Staff can attest to each policy with an electronic signature, and reminders chase anyone who hasn't. Approval workflows can be shaped per topic, with thresholds. Policies link to training, and an AI assistant answers employees' questions from your approved policies in many languages. If you have a compliance team that will run attestation campaigns and training across a large or multi-site workforce, that breadth is worth paying for, and Bindersnap doesn't offer it.

## Where Bindersnap fits better

- **It's built for healthcare surveys.** Our [requirements guides](/requirements) and [templates](/templates) are written for what CMS surveyors and accreditors ask small providers for, and the [required policies checklist](/tools/required-policies-checklist) shows which policies your facility needs.
- **You pay for writers, not readers.** $39 a month for each person who writes and publishes policies. Reviewers and staff who only read are free, and there's no long contract.
- **You can start today.** Create an account, upload the Word and PDF files you already have, and invite your approvers. The [getting started guide](/help/getting-started) shows how.
- **It does one thing completely.** A change to a policy is proposed, reviewed and approved before it becomes the policy, and every version keeps its approvals for as long as the binder exists. See [how a change becomes the record](/help/approvals).
- **Your record stays yours.** If you stop paying, you can still read and export every version and its approval history.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is PolicyTech still called PolicyTech?

NAVEX has renamed it NAVEX One Policy & Procedure Management. Its website still refers to it as "formerly NAVEX PolicyTech", and many people still search for it by the old name.

### Does NAVEX publish PolicyTech's prices?

Not as of October 8, 2026. Its pages offer "Request pricing" and a demo instead of a price list.

### Does Bindersnap do policy attestations?

Not today. Bindersnap records who **approved** each version of a policy. It doesn't collect staff read-and-sign attestations or link policies to training. See [policy attestation](/glossary/policy-attestation).

### Can I move from PolicyTech to Bindersnap?

Yes. Export your current policies as Word or PDF files and upload them. Each becomes a document with its own version history from that point on. Keep an export of your PolicyTech history, since earlier versions and their approvals don't move with the files.
