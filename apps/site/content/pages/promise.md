**Healthcare deserves software that works and a price that's fair. Bindersnap exists to give it both.**

## Healthcare is being bled dry

Ask anyone who runs a clinic, a surgery center or a home health agency about their software, and you'll hear the same two stories.

The first is that it doesn't work. The feature in the sales demo doesn't do what the demo showed. The workaround becomes the process. Feedback goes into a portal and disappears. Nurses and administrators already carry more than any job should ask of them, and they spend part of every week fighting their computers instead of caring for people.

The second is what it costs. Simple cloud software, the kind that stores documents and collects signatures, gets sold to healthcare in six- and seven-figure contracts. The price is a secret until a salesperson decides what you can afford. The contract runs for years. Then the feature everyone assumed was included turns out to be in a higher tier, and the bill goes up again.

That isn't a market. It's price gouging, and healthcare pays for it in staff it can't hire, equipment it can't buy and patients it can't serve.

## What we believe

- **Software should do what it says.** If we say Bindersnap does something, it does it, on the first day of your trial. If it doesn't do something, we tell you.
- **The price belongs on the website.** Not behind a demo, not in a quote, not in a negotiation.
- **Every customer gets every feature.** There's no premium tier holding back the thing you actually need.
- **Your records belong to you.** Nobody should be able to hold your approval history hostage to an invoice.
- **Simple beats sprawling.** Software that does one job completely is software that keeps working, and that a new administrator can learn in an afternoon.
- **The people who use it should be heard.** When something is wrong, the people who build Bindersnap should hear about it, not a ticket queue.

## What we promise you

- **One price, published.** $39 a month for each person who writes and publishes policies. Reviewers and staff who only read are free, in any number. See [pricing](/pricing).
- **No tiers, no setup fee, no sales call.** Every organization gets every feature, including the [editor](/editor). You start a 14-day trial without a card.
- **No long contract.** You subscribe month to month, and an owner can cancel at any time.
- **No surprise invoice.** Your bill changes only when you add or remove someone who writes policies. If our price ever changes, your owners hear about it by email at least 30 days before it applies, as our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) set out.
- **Reading and exporting stay free** whether or not you're subscribed. A surveyor's question doesn't wait for an invoice, and neither should your answer.
- **A straight line to the people who build it.** Send feedback from inside the app, or email me directly.

## Built for generations

I want the providers we serve to be around for generations: the rural clinic that's the only one for fifty miles, the hospice a family started, the surgery center that's still independent. That takes software they can afford to keep, year after year, without a renewal that doubles.

Every dollar a provider doesn't spend on overpriced software is a dollar for its nurses, its equipment and its patients. Across thousands of small providers, that's millions of dollars back in healthcare, where it belongs.

It also takes a company that will still be here. Bindersnap is priced to sustain itself honestly, on a price anyone can read, so we can keep this promise for as long as you need us.

Healthcare has been told for too long that this is just what software costs. It isn't. We're here to prove it.

David Gray, founder, [david@bindersnap.com](mailto:david@bindersnap.com)

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there features that cost extra?

No. Every organization gets every feature. The only thing that changes what you pay is how many people write and publish policies.

### Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. You subscribe month to month from the Billing page, and an owner can cancel at any time.

### Can the price go up?

If it ever does, your organization's owners hear by email at least 30 days before it applies to you, and can cancel before then.

### What happens to my policies if I stop paying?

They stay. Everyone can still read every policy and export it with its history. Nobody can propose, approve or publish new changes until someone subscribes.
