**Bindersnap does what this site says it does, and costs what the pricing page says it costs. Nothing more, nothing hidden.**

Ask anyone who runs a small healthcare provider about their software and you'll hear two complaints.

The first is that it doesn't work. The feature in the demo doesn't do what the demo showed. The workaround becomes the process. Feedback goes into a portal and nothing comes back. Nurses, administrators and compliance leads already carry more than any job should ask of them. They shouldn't have to fight their computer too.

The second is what it costs. Software for storing and approving documents gets sold the way hospital systems buy: a demo, a quote nobody else is allowed to see, a contract measured in years, and then an upsell for the feature everyone assumed was included. Every dollar that goes into a contract like that is a dollar that doesn't go to staff, training or patients.

Bindersnap is built to be the opposite of both.

## It works as advertised

We only describe what the product does today. If a page on this site says Bindersnap can do something, you can do it in your trial.

Where we don't do something, we say so. Our [comparisons](/compare) have a section on where the other products do more, and our answers say "not today" when that's the truth. Bindersnap doesn't collect staff read-and-sign attestations, for example, and the [PolicyTech comparison](/compare/policytech-alternative) says so in plain words.

When something breaks, tell us. **Send feedback** is in the app's sidebar on every page, and what you write goes straight to the people who build Bindersnap, not to a ticket queue run by somebody else. Or email the founder, David, at [david@bindersnap.com](mailto:david@bindersnap.com). A small provider's problem is the most important problem we have, because small providers are the only customers we have.

## One price, published, with nothing held back

Bindersnap costs $39 a month for each person who writes and publishes policies. Reviewers and staff who only read are free, in any number. That's the whole price list. See [pricing](/pricing).

- **No tiers.** Every organization gets every feature: as many binders and documents as it needs, approval rules, the full version history, the [editor](/editor), the audit packet and exports.
- **No setup fee, no implementation project and no sales call.** You create an account and start a 14-day trial with no card.
- **No long contract.** An owner can cancel at any time, and it takes effect at the end of the period already paid for.
- **No surprise invoice.** Your bill changes only when you add or remove someone who writes policies, and the People page shows what your paid seats cost. If we ever change the price, owners hear by email at least 30 days before it applies, as our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) set out, and can cancel first.
- **Your record isn't held hostage.** Reading and exporting stay free whether or not you're subscribed, for as long as your organization keeps its account. A surveyor's question doesn't wait for an invoice, and neither should your answer.

## Simple, on purpose

Bindersnap does one job: it keeps every policy's approved version, and the record of who approved it, ready for the surveyor. It doesn't try to be an HR system, a learning platform or a compliance suite, and it doesn't charge you for being one.

Simple software is software that keeps working. There's less to break, less to learn and less to pay for. A new administrator can pick it up in an afternoon, because the person who set it up isn't the only one who understands it.

## Built to last

We want the providers we serve to be around for generations: the rural clinic that's the only one for fifty miles, the hospice a family started, the surgery center that's still independent. That takes software they can afford to keep, year after year, without a renewal that doubles.

It also takes a company that will still be here. Bindersnap is priced so it pays for itself honestly, on a price you can read, not on a contract you have to negotiate. That's how we stay around to keep the promise.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there features that cost extra?

No. Every organization gets every feature. The only thing that changes what you pay is how many people write and publish policies.

### Do I have to sign a contract?

No. You subscribe monthly from the Billing page, and an owner can cancel at any time.

### What happens if something doesn't work?

Tell us with Send feedback in the app, or email david@bindersnap.com. It reaches the people who build Bindersnap.

### Can the price go up?

If it ever does, the owners of your organization hear by email at least 30 days before it applies to you, and can cancel before then.

### What happens to my policies if I stop paying?

They stay. Everyone can still read every policy and export it with its history. Nobody can propose, approve or publish new changes until someone subscribes.
