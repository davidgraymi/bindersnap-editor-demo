**$100 a month for your organization.** One plan, everything in it, and a 14-day free trial that starts without a card.

Most policy management software is built for hospital systems and sold the way they buy: a demo, a quote per user, a contract measured in years. A surgery center with forty staff or a home health agency with one administrator doesn't need any of that. It needs every policy in one place, each with its approvals on file, and the answer ready when a surveyor asks.

## What happens when the trial ends

Subscribe from the Billing page and nothing changes: your binders, versions and approval records stay exactly where they are. If you don't subscribe, **reading and exporting stay free, always.** You can still open every document and download it with its history. You can't propose or approve new changes until someone subscribes. Your record is never held hostage.

## Who it's for

Bindersnap is for small healthcare providers who keep a policy manual and answer to a surveyor or accreditor: [ambulatory surgery centers](/for/ambulatory-surgery-centers), [physician practices and clinics](/for/medical-practices), [rural health clinics](/for/rural-health-clinics), [home health agencies](/for/home-health-agencies), [hospices](/for/hospices), and [behavioral health programs](/for/behavioral-health). If your policies live in a shared drive and your sign-offs live in someone's inbox, it's for you.

Bindersnap holds your policies and procedures. It is not for patient records or other protected health information.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Bindersnap cost?

Bindersnap Pro costs $100 a month for your whole organization. There is one plan. Reviewers and people who only read your policies cost nothing extra.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new organization gets 14 days of Bindersnap Pro free, and you don't need a card to start. You subscribe from the Billing page when you're ready.

### Do I have to talk to sales?

No. You create an account, move your policies in, and subscribe with a card through Stripe. If you'd like help moving a large manual in, the [getting started guide](/help/getting-started) walks through it.

### What happens to my policies if we cancel?

They stay. Reading and exporting are free, always, so you can open any document, see who approved each version, and download it to PDF or Word whether or not you're subscribed.

### Can Bindersnap store patient information?

No. Bindersnap is for an organization's policies and procedures, not patient records. Keep protected health information in your clinical systems.
